## 9. The ten pivot moments, ranked

`[INFERENCE]` — the brief asked for 思想转折点 (points of ideological/thought change):

1. **Age 12, 1994/5 — "the diagnosis is a half-treatment."** The first real insight: medication worked *too well*, which meant she never learned what it didn't do. She calls herself *"a really good case study on what happens when you give a kid medication and nothing else."* This is the seed of her entire later argument. — https://sgutranscripts.org/wiki/SGU_Episode_962
2. **Age ~30–32 — the "brain went on strike."** Not a breakdown but an **executive-function stop**. She names it precisely: exhaustion, not inability. — https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=177809
3. **The life coach's "do nothing."** The first time she *subtracted* rather than added. — https://www.businessinsider.com/how-jessica-mccabe-used-adhd-strengths-build-dream-career-2024-1
4. **"I couldn't be successful and still be me. Spoilers: I was wrong."** The identity-level reframe. In the TEDx she is unambiguous that **she was the thing that needed to change**, and was wrong. — https://pickscribe.com/pl/v/JiwZQNYlGQI
5. **Meeting the tribe.** *"I didn't expect to learn: that I wasn't alone."* — same
6. **The channel was for her, not for them.** The decision to make videos public was *"in case they were helpful"* — i.e. she did not start an advocacy project. — https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=177809
7. **"This is a lie."** The collapse of the productivity promise — her own founding premise. — https://hackingyouradhd.com/podcast/how-to-adhd-with-jessica-mccabe
8. **Accepting the limits of tools** → hiring a housekeeper. *"No matter how many tools you have, the challenges are still there. I still don't have a clean car. I finally realized I need a housekeeper."* — https://www.additudemag.com/how-to-adhd-jessica-mccabe-interview/
9. **"I need Teresa."** Surrendering the do-it-alone myth. — same
10. **Motherhood resetting her content model.** *"My brain had literally been rewired."* — https://apexnews-sea.com/en/news/jessica-mccabe-built-how-to-adhd-by-solving-her-own-biggest-problem-2931b99f260628en_zz
