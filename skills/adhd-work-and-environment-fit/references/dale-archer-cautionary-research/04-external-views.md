## Q1. 十点量表有没有任何量表效度研究？有 APA/同行评议的验证吗？

# 🔴 明确结论：**没有。零。**

| 检索方向 | 结果 |
|---|---|
| "Dale Archer" + ten point scale / continuum + validity / psychometrics / clinical study | 仅返回 Fast Company 的 "**has yet to be proven in a clinical study**"，无一项验证研究 |
| "continuum theory of mental illness"（他自称 coined 的术语）+ peer reviewed | **该短语在任何学术文献中均不作为理论术语被使用**。检索命中的是另一条独立的、由 Peter et al. / Tomczyk et al. 发展的"continuum beliefs of mental illness"文献线（CB-MIS 量表，Psychological Medicine 2021;51(5):716–26; Soc Psychiatry Psychiatr Epidemiol 2022），**该文献线从未引用 Archer** |
| APA（American Psychiatric Association）层面 | **无任何关于该量表的 APA 认可、APA 指南引用或 APA 背书**。他的 "Distinguished Fellow of the APA" (2005) 是**个人荣誉**，与他提出的量表**无任何关系**，但公众常将两者混为一谈 |
| 精神科学会（CHADD / AACAP）指南 | **未检索到任何专业学会指南引用或背书 Archer 的十点量表** |

### ⚠️ 一个必须记录的**合流风险**（confluence risk）
`Continuum Beliefs of Mental Illness Scale (CB-MIS)`（Muehlan 等，德国；2024 心理测量学验证，N=1375）是一个**真正做过效度/信度/重测验证**的连续体信念量表。

🔴 **危险在于**：如果读者检索 "continuum beliefs mental illness scale validity"，会命中这个 CB-MIS，并误以为 Archer 的量表"有验证"。**这两者毫无关系。** Archer 的量表是 10 分制**自评**、无信度、无效度、无常模、无结构；CB-MIS 是 3 个分量表、5 点李克特、有 DRKS00023557 临床试验注册号。**记录此条以防 skill 后续被误引。**
- https://www.prophy.ai/article/186235182-Psychometric-validation-of-the-continuum-beliefs-of-mental-illness-scale-CB-MIS-and-its-associations-with-stigma/
- https://read.qxmd.com/read/35927343/continuum-beliefs-of-mental-illness-a-systematic-review-of-measures

### 📐 参照系：该领域"没有验证"的量表通常会经历什么
对照已被严肃处理的诊断工具，可直观看出 Archer 量表缺失了什么：
| | ASRS (Kessler 等) | AAP 2024 系统综述评估的工具 | **Archer 十点量表** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 发展样本 | 199 例，配 DSM-5 症状 | 逐项纳入 | **无** |
| 灵敏度/特异度 | 90% / 35%（Pettersson et al. 2018） | 报告 AUC 及区间 | **无** |
| 独立重复验证 | 多项 | **"no independent replication"** | **无** |
| 常模 | 有 | — | **无** |
| 重测信度 | 有 | **"test-retest reliability was not reported"** | **无** |
| 同行评议 | 数百次引用 | *Pediatrics* | **零次** |

- Harrison & Edwards (2023)：http://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/10870547231177470
- AAP (2024)：https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/153/4/e2024065854/196923

**【你推断的】**：ASRS 已被证明 PPV 在临床样本中"at best 61%, most fell below 20%"。Archer 的十点量表连 ASRS 的水准都达不到，因为**它连"努力测过一次"都没尝试过**。一个在主流 ADHD 临床中用于筛查的工具都不可靠，一个纯自评、无任何验证的分数被用来决定"你需不需要吃药"，**其风险等级不可估量**。

## Q2. 有没有同行评议论文**批评**这个 continuum 构想？

# 🟡 明确结论：**没有任何一篇论文点名批评 Archer。**

**但这不代表框架获得了学界背书**——恰恰相反，检索显示他**根本不在学术对话里**。

### 2.1 对 continuum 构想的学术批评是**存在的**，但针对的是整个类别，不点名

#### (a) 类别 vs 连续体的实证争议仍未解决，且两派都有硬证据
> "On one hand, evidence on the epidemiology of schizophrenia phenotypes suggests symptom phenotypes **may not be bound by conventional diagnostic thresholds** … **supporting continuum viewpoints**. On the other hand, evidence on the population structure suggests **a latent categorical structure; the population may be composed of two types of people.** However, both sets of evidence are beset by methodological limitations…"
> — Linscott & van Os, *Annual Review of Clinical Psychology* (2010) 6:391-419，PMID 20192792
> https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/20192792

🔴 **对 skill 的意义**：Archer 把 continuum 当作**已确立的事实**使用（"he coined the term 'continuum theory of mental illness'"、"these traits occur on a continuum rather than as a simple on-off switch"），而 2010 年的**权威综述明确说这个争议根本没有解决**。

#### (b) 2026 年最新综述：DSM 分类"still lack validity"，但 continuum 也救不了
> "**DSM classifications still lack validity.** The continuum model supports preclinical stages but **has no discrete conversions to different stages of illness.**"
> — van der Gaag et al., *Early Intervention in Psychiatry*, 2026;20(7), DOI 10.1111/eip.70185
> https://research.vu.nl/en/publications/the-staging-model-in-psychosis-and-preventive-interventions-an-em/

🔴 **这是截至 2026-10 最新的、我要到的最关键学术信号**：continuum 模型**可以解释前驱期，但无法给出"何时该治疗"的切分**——而这恰恰是 Archer 十点量表声称要解决的问题（"9 分以上可能需要药"）。**学术界明确指出 continuum 缺乏这个能力，而 Archer 正是靠这个能力吃饭。**

#### (c) 方法论警告：不能从一个层面推断另一个层面
> "Complex systems lend themselves to analysis at multiple levels of organization, and we should be wary of **inferring from a continuum at one level that there must also be a continuum at another.**"
> — Fernyhough, *Psychology Bulletin* — https://dx.doi.org/10.1093/SCHBUL/SBZ002

#### (d) 一般发育理论的反向推理：滑向分布底部 ≠ 优势
> "**A better fit is to suggest that those who do not suffer from ADHD clearly have an adaptive and likely selective advantage across multiple generations given that they represent the current norm.** … readers … will quickly conclude that **falling to the bottom of a normal curve in the development of self-control rather than representing an advantage represents a disadvantage.**"
> — Sam Goldstein（临床心理学家，专栏文章）— https://samgoldstein.com/resources/articles/general/adhd-as-an-advantageadaptation-revisited.aspx

### 2.2 检索层面的"沉默"应如何解读
🔴 **【你推断的】**：名字被学术文献点名批评是需要"值得被批评的分量"，Archer 达不到这个门槛。**他的框架的学术处境不是"被反驳"，而是"未被纳入"**——它活在商业畅销书、YouTube 式自媒体摘要和媒体转述中，**从未进入期刊对话**。对 skill 而言这更重要：**它无法被引证，也无法被证伪，所以无法被修正。**

## Q3. 有没有学者指出「5-8 是甜蜜点」是事后归因 / 选择性样本？

# 🔴 有。而且**不止一位**，批评精确到机制层面。

### 3.1 最精准的一句：直接命中"选择性样本"机制

> "Research on the association between ADHD and creativity has yielded mixed results, and **the case for ADHD as a gift seems speculative and selective. It tempts advocates off the scientific high ground**, as when Koretsky cites a group of high achievers as representative of this condition. **What of the school drop-outs, the impulsive jail population, and the vast majority between these two extremes?**"
> — *Psychology Today*，《Should We Welcome the "Gift" of ADHD — or Return it?》(2013)
> https://www.psychologytoday.com/au/blog/aspergers-alive/201310/should-we-welcome-the-gift-adhd-or-return-it

🔴 **这一句同时命中 Archer 的三个操作**：
- "**speculative and selective**" → 直接指认选择偏倚
- "What of the school drop-outs, **the impulsive jail population**…" → 指出被丢弃的分母。⚠️ **尖锐的巧合**：Archer 本人 1988–1995 就是 Phelps Correctional Institute 的监狱精神科医生、并在刑事法庭担任数百次 sanity/competency 专家证人（Wikipedia）——**他比大多数人更清楚被丢掉的"分母"里有什么**。
- "tempts advocates **off the scientific high ground**" → 直接指出这已不是科学主张。

### 3.2 最定量的一句：近 4000 项研究的普查式否定

> "**In the nearly 4,000 peer reviewed, scientific studies dealing with ADHD over the past thirty years, there hasn't been a single study in which the group with ADHD performed better on a valuable trait, asset or behavior than those without ADHD.** This is not to moralize, demonize, nor pathologize children with ADHD. It is to suggest that being delayed in the development of self-control in our complex society is **no picnic.** … I do not believe we need to create myths or 'Just So Stories' to help children with ADHD and their families feel better about this impairing condition."
> — Sam Goldstein，同上

🔴 **必须并列记录的诚实**：Goldstein 的措辞（"Just So Stories" 借自 Kipling）是**故意不友善**的。他反对的不是"帮助家庭感觉好些"，而是**用未验证的叙事替换治疗**。这与 Arch 的支持者（Marilyn Wedge、Allen Frances）属于**同一场"去污名化"运动的两端**——Wedge 说 Archer "moves psychiatry a step in the right direction"，Goldstein 说这是 Kipling 式的 Just So Story。**同一个动作，两种评价。**

### 3.3 同文内对 Archer 论点的直接点名（研究型）
> "Recently, the continuum model has come under pressure from different directions. **Its postulation of gradual quantitative transitions across a continuum of symptom expression lends itself well to empirical evaluation.**" 〔然而并没有人做这个评估〕
> — Fernyhough, *Psychology Bulletin*

### 3.4 当代神经多样性阵营内部的自相矛盾（最新、最有力）

**Sarah Greenberg**（Understood.org 持证心理治疗师）—— **注意：她是在那篇批评 Archer 的 Fast Company 文章里被采访的**：
> "**Rather than viewing the condition on a continuum**, Greenberg sees neurodiversity as creating '**jagged profiles**,' meaning many with neurological differences overperform in some areas and underperform in others."
> — https://www.fastcompany.com/91482666/adhd-a-challenge-a-superpower-or-both

🔴 **她提出的替代模型比 Archer 的更精确，且是可操作的**：不是一条平滑的连续体，而是**锯齿状（jagged）剖面**——同一个人在 A 域远超均值、B 域远低于均值。**这个模型不需要"甜蜜点"这个概念，直接解构了它。**

**Megan Anna Neff**（临床心理学家，Neurodivergent Insights 创始人）—— 同文：
> "Neff **believes ADHD should be classified as a disability** so that those who need the support, resources and **legal protections** can continue to access them."
> 🔴 与 Allen Frances 序言里的 "redefines all but the most severe cases of ADHD as **expectable difference, not medical diagnosis**" **直接冲突**。Neff 是从**残障权利/社会模型**内部提出的反驳，因此 Archer 的去病理化举措在理论上反而**削弱了她想保护的东西**。

**神经多样性社区的自我批评**：
> "The superpower narrative, while more positive in tone, **implies that the right kind of ADHD is actually an advantage, which slides into suggesting that people who are just struggling, without the compensatory giftedness, are failing to properly leverage their condition.**"
> — https://neurolaunch.com/adhd-ableism/

### 3.5 后果预测（临床从业者的具体推演）
> "**Does Framing ADHD as a Superpower Cause People to Avoid Getting Treatment? This is the most legitimate criticism of the superpower narrative, and it deserves a real answer rather than a dismissive one.** The short version: it can, if the framing is irresponsible. **If someone walks away believing their ADHD is simply a gift that requires no management, they may skip therapy, avoid medication that would meaningfully improve their functioning**, and chalk up every difficulty to external factors rather than addressing underlying patterns."
> — https://neurolaunch.com/adhd-is-a-superpower/

## Q4.「9 分以上通常需要药物」这个说法有无证据基础？

# 🔴 明确结论：**无任何证据基础。**

### 4.1 出处全部追到"hypothesis"或记者转述
| 出处 | 原话 | 性质 |
|---|---|---|
| Fast Company 2026 | "a hypothesis **that has yet to be proven in a clinical study**" | 记者定性为假说 |
| Fast Company 2026 | "those who score nine or higher are likely to struggle in everyday life and **may require** medication" | 情态动词 may |
| BestOfSwla 2015 | "Those with a 9, 10 or 10+, **he hypothesized**, probably needed treatment and medication" | 记者用 hypothesized + probably |

🔴 **注意 Fast Company 内部的不一致**：同一篇文章里，记者在**总述处**写 "a hypothesis that has yet to be proven"，但在**细节处**却写成陈述句 "those who score nine or higher **are likely to** struggle"。**记者在中段悄悄把假说写成了事实**，这是二手传播链失真的活标本。

### 4.2 该阈值同时与**现行指南相反**
临床实践指南中的 treatment intensity 由 **DSM-5 的症状计数、功能损害程度、病程、领域广度、共同病症**决定，**不存在任何 9/10 这样的分界点**。Archer 的框架反过来主张"12 项中 6 项即诊断"的 DSM 计数逻辑过于武断——**然后用自己一个同样武断的数字替换了它，且更武断**（因为它连条目结构都没有）。

### 4.3 一旦把"分数 → 用药"作为行动指令，风险被放大
🔴 一个无法验证的分数被用作**用药决策的依据**，这是本 skill 中伦理风险最高的一点。而 2025 年的新证据显示，这个"分数决定一切"的框架的受害者群体是有明确证据的：

**Holden & Kobayashi-Wood（St Andrews / Durham），*Scientific Reports* 15(1):20945, 2025-07-01，DOI 10.1038/s41598-025-04782-y**（n=28 名晚期确诊女性，混合方法调查）：
> "Results starkly demonstrate the **criticism and lack of support** participants faced from society and medical professionals… Participants commonly reported **internalising criticism and described disconcertingly low self-esteem; citing guilt, shame, and negative self-perception** due to delayed diagnoses. Participants found diagnosis **revelatory**, their lives finally making sense… Participants reflected on 'what could have been', and **described grieving the lives they could have led if diagnosed earlier.**"
> — https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC12218314 ｜PMID 40594310

**二手博客对本研究的引申（⚠️ 需注明是引申非原文）**：
> "**Not one of them described their ADHD as a superpower.**"
> — https://www.adult-autism-assessment.com/post/adhd-is-not-a-superpower-and-calling-it-one-is-making-things-worse
> ⚠️ 我核对了原始摘要，**这句话不是 Holden & Kobayashi-Wood 的原文结论**，是该博客的推论。摘要中没有这句话。记录时不应引作研究结论。
> 该博客基于该研究提出的**机制推论**（逻辑独立成立）："**If ADHD is a superpower, then struggling with ADHD means you're failing to use your superpower correctly.** When the dominant cultural narrative says 'ADHD is a gift,' the person who is drowning in their ADHD doesn't conclude that the narrative is wrong. **They conclude that they're broken in a way that goes beyond even their diagnosis.**"

🔴 **时间巧合值得记录**：Better Than Normal 写于 2010；这批评"优势叙事让晚期确诊者更晚求助"的研究发表于 **2025 年 7 月**——**相隔 15 年，正好覆盖 Archer 全部公共影响力期。**

## Q5. 他"自己不吃药"的立场，批评方怎么说？

### 5.1 Barkley —— 最强硬、最具体、且直接针对其逻辑结构

**Barkley 的核心反驳（PsychotherapySphere 转录，Barkley 本人对 Center for ADHD Awareness, Canada 讲话）**：
> "**Many people may be gifted and talented in various aspects of these other human abilities, but never attribute that giftedness or that success to the AD/HD itself** … I want people to understand that while people might be gifted and talented and successful **in spite of** their AD/HD, **it is not because of** their AD/HD. **The AD/HD itself may, in fact, make you less effective than other people who have equally talented areas in those human abilities.**"
> — https://psychotherapysphere.com/tag/adhd/

🔴 **这一击的精妙之处**：Barkley 攻击的不是"ADHD 有优点"，而是**归因方向**。成功者用自己的技能+环境支持克服了障碍 → 所以障碍本身**可能使他们比同等天赋者效率更低**。这直接命中 Archer 序章的名句 "not **in spite of** their ADHD **but because of** it"。

**Barkley 的政治论证（比临床论证更致命）**：
> "**It is going to be very hard for society to take you all seriously if you continue to trumpet this disorder as a gift.** … **There is no way that we can go to Ottawa and walk the halls of Parliament arguing for accommodations, entitlements, funding of AD/HD medications on the one hand, while rah-rah cheering AD/HD as this wonderful giftedness that we have and you don't.** … **What's worse than negative thinking? Falsely boosting people's self-esteem with unsubstantiated claims that skew public understanding of the disorder. There's a real danger that attitudes on AD/HD could go from, 'There's no such thing,' directly to, 'Why should we make accommodations for your son? Doesn't he has the gift of AD/HD?'**"
> — 同上（Jonathan Miller 转录，2013）

🔴 **这段话是对 Archer 全套方案的结构性反驳**：把 5–8 讲成优势，**就取消了重度患者争取资源的道德与法律基础**。而这正是 Neff 2026 年仍在说的问题（见 §3.4）。

**Barkley 谈 hyperfocus 的祛魅**（直击 Archer 最核心的机制）：
> "While 'hyperfocus' is often touted as a benefit, it is **frequently directed at non-essential tasks, leading to the neglect of critical life responsibilities.** … **the lack of 'inhibitory control' often prevents these connections from being distilled into useful outcomes.**"
> — https://thehumanlogic.org/archives/1054

### 5.2 Barkley 也用 Archer 用的同一批人，但得出相反结论
*Library Journal* 对 Barkley《12 Principles for Raising a Child with ADHD》(2020) 的书评：
> "**Barkley disagrees with experts who label ADHD a 'gift,' contending that ADHD cannot both be a gift and a serious disability that requires services.** At the same time, **the author cites success stories of those such as TV star Ty Pennington and Olympic swimmer Michael Phelps, both of whom have the condition.** Good parenting was essential to the achievements of these famous faces, the author explains, as **Phelps's family taught him to adopt routines and Pennington was guided to choose a career that took advantage of his unique strengths.**"
> — https://www.libraryjournal.com/review/12-principles-for-raising-a-child-with-adhd

🔴🔴 **这是本次调研最有价值的一张对照表**：Archer 与 Barkley **引用了同一批案例人物**。

| | Dale Archer | Russell Barkley |
|---|---|---|
| Pennington 的成功归因于 | 其 ADHD 特征被善用（"brain was made for chaos and multitasking"） | **父母/导师的引导**（"guided to choose a career that took advantage of his unique strengths"） |
| Phelps 的成功归因于 | ADHD 赋能的韧性 | **家庭教他建立常规**（"Phelps's family taught him to adopt routines"） |
| 关键论断 | not in spite of it, **but because of it** | successful **in spite of** it, **not because of it** |
| 结论 | 停止用药 | 用药是 "the predominant treatment approach" |

**同一批人，相反的结论，取决于谁在解释。** 这本身就是"选择性与选择性归因"问题的最佳教学案例。

### 5.3 来自 Archer 自己受访者的反向材料 ⭐

**David Neeleman（捷蓝创始人，Archer 书中重点案例）**——**注意：Neeleman 的这些表述发生在 Archer 的书之前，且独立于 Archer**：
> "**My wife can't always figure out what the heck I am thinking, and my kids want me to focus on just one thing with them.** I find it difficult. It's hard for me to do the mundane things in life. **I have an easier time planning a 20-aircraft fleet than I do paying the light bill.**"
> "…**life is full of trade-offs, and living with my untreated ADD is one of them.**"
> — https://www.greatschools.org/gk/articles/5-top-execs-add/

> "**I have no idea what I'm doing one day to the next.**" ／ 靠助理写信管日程
> — 同上

**2023 年 Forbes 专访（他 60 岁前后）**：
> **Maya Richard-Craven: "Do you feel like ADHD has held you back in any way?"**
> **David Neeleman: "When I finally figured out I had it, it was a relief. I was just really careful to surround myself with people that could complement my ADHD."**
> — https://www.forbes.com/sites/mayarichard-craven/2023/09/28/jetblue-founder-david-neeleman-opens-up-about-having-adhd/

🔴🔴 **这是本次调研最尖锐的一个发现**：
1. **Neeleman 从未自称处在"5–8 甜蜜点"**。那是 Archer 的归类。Neeleman 自己从未使用这个框架。
2. Neeleman 描述的**核心生存策略是"hire people who complement me"**——**用外部人力结构替代自己的执行功能**。这恰恰是 Barkley 的"externalize"模型，**不是 Archer 的"利用你的 bingo brain"模型**。
3. Archer 把 Neeleman 收进"优势案例"，而 **Neeleman 本人把这些经历描述为 trade-off、relationships 的损耗、以及需要助手"steer me from appointment to appointment, keeping me on track"**。

### 5.5 来自普通读者/家长的一线批评
> "**Dr Archer's assertion that ADHD is a gift, an advantage while ignoring the realities on the ground is unscientific, potentially dangerous and very simplistic.** … **At times I felt like I was reading the diaries of the rich and famous who have the means to make certain types of big changes while ordinary people do not.** The chapter on schools seemed so superficial.. **Has the author been in a typical city public school?** They look nothing like the one he described…"
> — karen59_1（NetGalley 书评，2/5 星，与作者同姓但声明无亲属关系）
> https://www.everand.com/audiobook/638424914/The-ADHD-Advantage-What-You-Thought-Was-a-Diagnosis-May-Be-Your-Greatest-Strength

🔴 **这条批评触及一个未被任何学者提及但结构性关键的点**：Archer 的方法论是**用他自己的样本（他能接触的：诊所病人、私人朋友、名人、他的孩子）来推导普遍规律**。他的"成功案例"几乎全部是**有资源、有助理、有合伙人、有家庭兜底的人**——而这些恰恰是分母里最特殊的那一层。**样本选择偏差不是"学术不严谨"，而是"他的样本就是他的社交圈"。**

### 5.6 来自左翼/劳动阶层的反向批评（最容易被忽略但很有价值）
> "**Most such criticisms are written by journalists, physicians, and other white-collar professionals with little working-class experience. I contend that economic issues loom large here.** Many children from overstuffed classrooms graduate into employment in overstuffed factories or cube farms. **The skills we claim kids should learn in school take second place to real workplace skills like shutting up and buckling down. We diagnose kids, and increasingly adults, on flimsy grounds and questionable science, to subordinate them.**"
> — Kevin L Nenstiel, "The ADHD Revolution", 2015-07
> http://wordbasket.blogspot.com/2015/07/the-adhd-revolution.html

🔴 **这是对 Archer 整个批评方向的一次反转**：Archer 说"别给孩子贴 ADHD 标签，是为了让他们的 ADHD 特质成为优势"；Nenstiel 说"贴这个标签本身就是一种**规训**，因为学校和职场本就不为坐不住的人设计"。**这两者在政治上站在同一侧（反学校、反药企），却在结论上对立**——因为一个把问题归因于**诊断制度**，一个把问题归因于**学习方式不匹配**。**这不是"谁对谁错"，而是同一批判的两种路线，而 Archer 的路线有一个致命弱点：他同时接受了"制度压迫 ADHD 儿童"的前提，和"ADHD 特质本身是优势"的前提，而 Barkley 指出的恰恰是这两个前提不能同时最大化。**

### 5.7 结构性盲区：注意力不集中型（ADHD-I）被排除
> "My daughter was diagnosed with **innatentive type** ADHD… **He doesn't even include this type of ADHD.** I kept reading and learned a lot but **I can't believe he leaves out an entire population.** And when he tells a story of a child giving up ballet as it's not fast paced enough and recommends ballet two pages later — I had it. **Enough.**"
> — Goodreads 书评（2019-11-17）
> https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/23398983-the-adhd-advantage

🔴 **这是十点量表最具体的机制缺陷**：一个**以"好动、冒险、多任务、危机中冷静"为优势特征**的量表，**在构造上就无法处理没有这些特征的 ADHD-I**。而流行病学上女性、成人多为 ADHD-I。**量表把"没病"编码成了"低分"，因此系统性漏掉了被误诊人群中的一大块——恰好是 2025 年那项研究（28 名晚期确诊女性）所研究的人群。**

## 🔑 对 skill 的一句判断
> Dale Archer 的十点量表是**一个听起来合理、流传很广、被主流商业媒体转述过、但从未被任何形式的验证触及过的框架**。它的所有具体阈值（≤4 / 5–8 / 9–10+）都在自媒体与 AI 摘要工具中被反复复述为既定事实，而其唯一权威背书者（Allen Frances）本人是**反对 DSM 化与过度诊断的 DSM-IV 任务组主席，而非 ADHD 专业学会**。
> **它同时具备"可引用（有名有姓有畅销书）、可传播（一句话可复述）、无法证伪（无量表、无研究、无反对论文）"三个特征——这正是高风险伪框架的标准画像。**
> 同时必须诚实记录：**这个 skill 若采用他的框架，就必须同时携带 §1（零验证）与 §3（选择性样本）两条批注，否则它就变成了伪科学放大器。**
