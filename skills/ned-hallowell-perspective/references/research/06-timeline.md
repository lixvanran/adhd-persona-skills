## 2. The Formative Decision: 1981

| Year | Event | Conf |
|---|---|---|
| **1981** | **Begins treating ADHD patients.** Attends a lecture on children with ADHD and recognises himself as having it. | ●●● |

- **[O]** *"In 1981, Hallowell attended a lecture about children with ADHD and stated that it was there when he learned that he has the syndrome. Despite this, he has never been officially diagnosed. For the next ten years Hallowell diagnosed people with it using the Conner's Scale."*
  Source: https://peoplepill.com/people/edward-hallowell (⚠️ aggregator; cites encyclopedia.com)
- **[H]** Contradicting framing: *"my two so-called learning disabilities got diagnosed after I'd finished all my training. I discovered that I had both dyslexia and ADHD."* Source: orionsmethod.com
- **⚠️ CENTRAL UNRESOLVED CONTRADICTION:** His own website says he *"has ADHD and dyslexia himself"* (drhallowell.com). A biographical aggregator says he was *"never officially diagnosed."* **Both cannot be literally true.** Must be preserved in any downstream use.
- **[I] Why 1981 matters:** it is the load-bearing date of his entire career. His method, his authority, and his commercial product all derive from a self-recognition that happened after his training and that he has never, in anything I retrieved, subjected to formal diagnostic testing.
- **[I]** Note also: the recognition came at a **lecture on children**. His entire adult identity reorganised around a paediatric condition he discovered by overhearing other people's children.

## 4. ⭐ THE FOUR IDEOLOGICAL TURNING POINTS

These are the years where **the framework itself changed**, not just the output.

### 1994 — Deficit → Advantage
*Driven to Distraction* establishes the strength-based model. Everything after this is downstream.
- **[I]** Before 1994 he was a Harvard academic writing about children and families. After 1994 he is a public author whose product is the reframe itself. **This is the founding pivot.**

### 2011 — "Gift" reclassified as a mischaracterization
At CHADD, both men declare the gift-vs-curse debate a false frame and substitute **"dimensional disorder."**
- **[I]** **The most consequential strategic move in the corpus.** It preserves every practical payoff of the gift framing while making it unfalsifiable — because if ADHD is a dimension, no adverse outcome is attributable to ADHD per se.
- **⚠️ CONTRADICTION:** his publisher bio for *Driven to Distraction* still carries *"I don't treat disabilities; I help unwrap gift!"* — the 2011 rebrand and the 1994 brand both remain live.

### 2020–21 — Trait, not disorder (VAST)
- **[H]** The diagnostic term is declared inaccurate and stigmatizing; VAST replaces it.
- **[I]** This is an **escalation, not a correction.** The content is the 1994 thesis with new vocabulary. Notably, VAST also **expands the population** to include people without ADHD whose environment produces the phenotype — which widens the addressable market while softening the pathology.

### 2023–24 — Neurodiversity vocabulary, unchanged content
- **[H]** His official bio now reads *"through his strength-based approach to neurodiversity."* Source: drhallowell.com
- **[I]** Same thesis, contemporary framing. **No doctrinal change in thirty years.** The turning point is that there wasn't one.
